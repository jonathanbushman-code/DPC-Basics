Our Solution

Direct Primary Care removes the barriers between you and your doctor, delivering better care at a fraction of the cost.

What is Direct Primary Care?

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a healthcare model where patients pay a simple monthly membership fee for comprehensive primary care services — no insurance billing, no copays, no surprise charges.

More Time With Your Doctor

Because we see fewer patients, your visits are longer and more thorough. No more feeling rushed through a 7-minute appointment.

Always Reachable

You'll have your doctor's email and phone number. Many illnesses can be diagnosed and treated with a simple conversation — no office visit needed.

Virtual Visits

Get care from anywhere with telemedicine visits. Video, phone, and text-based consultations are included in your membership.

Healthcare Is Broken. We're Fixing It.

The traditional healthcare system is built around insurance companies, not patients. We flipped the script.

The Problem

  • Average doctor visit lasts 7 minutes
  • Wait weeks or months for an appointment
  • Surprise bills and confusing insurance claims
  • Doctors juggling 2,500+ patients
  • Impersonal, assembly-line care
  • Can't reach your doctor when you need them

Our Solution

  • 30–60 minute appointments, every time
  • Same-day and next-day availability
  • One flat monthly fee — no surprises
  • Small patient panels for personalized care
  • A doctor who knows your name and story
  • Call, text, or email your doctor anytime

What's Included in Your Membership

Your monthly membership covers a wide range of primary care services with no additional charges.

Unlimited Office Visits

Visit as often as you need — annual physicals, sick visits, chronic disease management, and more.

Preventive Care

Wellness exams, health screenings, immunizations, and personalized prevention plans to keep you healthy.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, asthma, and other chronic conditions.

Acute Illness & Injury

Colds, flu, infections, minor injuries, lacerations, sprains — get treated quickly without the ER.

Discounted Labs & Meds

Wholesale pricing on blood work, medications, and imaging. Know the cost before the service is rendered.

Care Coordination

When you need a specialist, we'll coordinate referrals and help navigate the healthcare system on your behalf.
