Why Direct Primary Care?

No insurance hassles. No copays. No surprise bills. Just you and your doctor, focused entirely on your health.

24/7 Physician Access

Call, text, or email your doctor whenever you need. Illness doesn't respect office hours, and neither do we.

No Insurance Required

Cut out the third-party restrictions. We see fewer patients so we can deliver more personalized, thorough care.

Transparent Pricing

Know costs before services are rendered. Discounted medications, labs, and imaging at prices that make sense.

Unlimited Visits

See your doctor as often as you need with no copays and no per-visit fees. Same-day and next-day appointments available.

Home Visits

When you can't come to us, we'll come to you. Home visits available for members who need care at their doorstep.

Discounted Services

Cash-pay pricing on medications, blood tests, and radiology. Know what you'll pay before you receive care.
